Hand a goal to a lead agent and keep the decisions. Tavern's Keeper scouts with a small team, proposes a quest map for your approval and delegates the work to builders and a checker. Keep steering in one thread rather than carrying messages between agents.

## Follow the work

The common room brings together what needs you, quests, side quests and solos. Each quest has a map, party, Notebook, rules, decisions and history. Open a member's report in the side panel, answer an ask in place and read the returned tale for verified results, credits and work left for you.

Five classes separate leading, scouting, building, checking and bounded side work. Characters add names, crests and short presentation lines, not permissions or expertise. A separate checker is labelled as same-family when an independent model-family check cannot be established.

## A journal in every thread

The Journal checklist has steps, blockers, evidence and grouped Undo. Linked work needs evidence, and only an assigned Warden can verify or reject it. Journal reminders do not start agent turns. Keepers' board quests remain separate from these personal checklists.

## You keep control

Explicit plan approval is the default. Standing rules stay local to one Keeper unless you make them global. Scope presets constrain the work; Pi can use a pre-execution guard, while an approved detection-only exception on other providers can notice violations but does not prevent them. Production changes and other consequential actions still require your consent.

Settings controls pacing, providers, notifications and presentation. Automatic pacing tuning is off by default. Inspect recorded changes, pin values, transfer settings as TOML and save private backups from the page or `bb tavern`. Insights shows recorded usage costs when Burn is installed. There is no hard dollar cap; these are ordinary provider-backed bb threads.
