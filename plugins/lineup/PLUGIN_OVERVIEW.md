Pick a model from a short list instead of scrolling or searching through every provider and model bb knows about, without leaving bb's own model menu.

## What you get

- A **Lineup** tab, a pin icon at the start of bb's model menu, before its provider tabs. When only one provider is enabled and bb draws no tabs, Lineup adds the row with an **All models** tab for bb's own list.
- The tab shows the models you pinned, in your order, and under them the 3 models your threads used most recently that you have not pinned. Recent models come from your thread history, so they are there from the first day.
- Hover a model in any list, including bb's own, and click the pin where its check mark goes to add it or take it out. A pinned model always shows a filled pin. On a touch screen every row shows its pin.
- Picking a row switches the composer's model just as picking it in bb's list does, and the menu stays open so you can change the reasoning level too.
- Typing in the search box filters your pins, shows every recent model that matches (up to **Models in Recent** in the plugin's settings, 25 by default) and lists matching models from every provider underneath, so you can find and pin any model without changing tabs.
- **Tab** and **Shift+Tab** in the search box switch between Lineup and bb's list; the arrow keys and Enter work as in bb's list.
- The menu opens on the list you used last. Until you choose Lineup it opens on bb's own list, as it always has.
- **Settings → Plugins → Lineup** reorders the lineup, removes models from it and adds any model with bb's own picker.
- It works in the New thread composer and in a thread's composer, on the desktop and on a phone, where bb opens the model menu as a drawer at the bottom of the screen. The lineup and the last tab are saved on the bb server, so every window shares them.

## Limits

- bb has no plugin interface for its model menu, so Lineup adds its tabs to the menu bb 0.47 draws. If a bb update changes that menu, the tabs stop appearing until Lineup is updated; bb's own menu keeps working.
- Lineup does not change bb's own lists; it only adds its tab and the pins.
- A model that is not offered on this machine, for example because its provider is disabled, is left out of the recent models and shown as "not offered here" among your pins.
- Recent models come from at most the 400 most recently active threads, so a long-unused model can drop off before the limit is reached.
